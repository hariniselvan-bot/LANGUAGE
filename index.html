Frequently Asked Questions

How do live 1-on-1 lessons work? +
You can book 30-minute or 50-minute video calls directly with your chosen tutor inside the dashboard. All video tools, whiteboards, and notes are built directly into Stackly.
Can I change my tutor if I want a different teaching style? +
Yes! You can switch tutors at any time with zero hassle. Your progress notes and lesson history transfer automatically to your new instructor.
Are the language certificates internationally recognized? +
All Stackly certificates adhere strictly to CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) standards and can be shared on LinkedIn or submitted to employers.
What happens if I miss a scheduled class? +
You can cancel or reschedule any class up to 2 hours before start time without losing your credit.
