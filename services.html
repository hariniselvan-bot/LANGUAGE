FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our language learning services.

How do the 1-on-1 tutoring sessions work? +
You can book 30-minute or 50-minute video sessions with your chosen tutor through our dashboard. All lessons include digital whiteboards, shared notes, and real-time feedback. Sessions are recorded for your review.
Can I switch tutors if I want a different teaching style? +
Absolutely! You can switch tutors at any time with zero hassle. Your progress notes and lesson history transfer automatically to your new instructor.
Are the language certificates internationally recognized? +
Yes! All Stackly certificates adhere to CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) standards and are recognized by employers and universities worldwide.
What happens if I miss a scheduled class? +
You can cancel or reschedule any class up to 2 hours before the start time without losing your credit. Late cancellations may incur a fee.
Do you offer refunds if I'm not satisfied? +
We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you're not satisfied with your first week, we'll provide a full refund — no questions asked.
