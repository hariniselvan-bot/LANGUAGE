How do 1-on-1 private lessons work on Stackly? +
You select your preferred tutor, pick a available time slot on their calendar, and join the live video session right in your browser dashboard.
What internet requirements do I need for video lessons? +
A stable broadband connection with at least 5 Mbps download/upload speed and a working webcam and microphone are recommended.
Can I pause or cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes! You can pause your subscription for up to 60 days or cancel anytime from your Account Settings with zero cancellation fees.
Are lessons available in my timezone? +
With tutors located across 40+ countries in Americas, Europe, Asia, and Africa, live lessons are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
